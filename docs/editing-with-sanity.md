# Editing the Alpha Hardscapes website

Your website reads its text, photos and settings from a content editor called **Sanity Studio**. You edit in the Studio, press **Publish**, and the live site updates within a couple of minutes. No code, no developer needed.

**Where:** `https://alphahardscapes.com/studio` (sign in with the Google account or email TTR Digital invited).

Everything you change is saved as a draft until you press **Publish**, so you can take your time.

---

## The sidebar

| Item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Site settings** | Phone number, email, hours, social links, service area line, the green button text, the message at the bottom of every page, the licence line in the footer |
| **Home page** | Headline, hero photo, the three trust points and numbers, the "why us" items, the five process steps, which services and projects are featured |
| **About page** | Headline, intro, Sebastian's photo and bio, the "Alpha advantage" items |
| **Services** | One entry per service (Patios & Walkways, Retaining Walls, Outdoor Kitchens…) |
| **Projects & gallery** | One entry per job: photos, title, town, materials, before/after |
| **Reviews** | Customer reviews shown on the home page, the Reviews page and in sidebars |
| **FAQs** | The questions on the FAQ page (and up to five on the home page) |
| **Service areas** | County pages and town pages (Cheshire, Southington, Avon…) |
| **Blog posts** | Articles under /blog/ |
| **Other pages** | Privacy policy and terms |

## How to change text on a page

1. Open the item in the sidebar (for example **Services → Paver Patios & Walkways**).
2. Click into the field and type. Fields are labelled in plain English ("Page heading", "Intro paragraph", "Main text").
3. In **Main text** you can make headings, bullet lists, bold text and links with the toolbar above the text box.
4. Press **Publish** (bottom right). Done.

To undo, open the **⋮** menu next to Publish and choose **Discard changes** (before publishing) or **Restore** a previous version (after).

## How to add a project with photos

1. Sidebar → **Projects & gallery** → **+** (top).
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: say what it is, e.g. *"Bluestone patio with fire pit"* (not "Project 12").
   - **URL slug**: click **Generate** next to it.
   - **Town**: e.g. *Cheshire, CT*. This shows on the card and helps Google connect you with that town.
   - **Services**: pick the services this job shows. The gallery filters use this.
   - **Summary**: one or two sentences, what the problem was and what you built.
   - **Cover photo**: the best photo. Drag it in or click **Select**. Add **Alt text** (one sentence describing the photo).
   - **More photos**: add as many as you like. Phone photos are fine; the site resizes them.
   - **Before / After photos** (optional): when both are set, the page shows a drag-to-compare slider.
   - **Materials used**: e.g. *Techo-Bloc Blu 60*, *Thermal bluestone*.
   - **Feature on the home page**: turn on for your best six.
3. Press **Publish**.

**Photo tips:** landscape (horizontal) photos look best as covers. Avoid photos with house numbers or faces unless the customer agreed. Up to about 10 MB per photo.

## How to change the phone number (or hours, email, social links)

1. Sidebar → **Site settings** → **Contact & hours** tab.
2. **Phone (display)** is what people see, e.g. `(475) 227-6896`. **Phone (for links)** is what phones dial: `+14752276896`. Change both.
3. Press **Publish**. The header, footer, mobile call bar, contact page and the business information Google reads all update.

Do the same for **Email**, **Hours**, Facebook, Instagram and the Google links.

---

## How to add a review

Sidebar → **Reviews** → **+**. Enter the customer's name, the review text and stars. Paste the link to the Google review if you have it. Turn on **Feature on the home page** for the three you want there. Publish.

---

## How to add a town page

Sidebar → **Service areas** → **+**. Set **Type** to *Town*, choose the **County**, write a short intro that mentions the town and what you build there, add a photo of a job in that town if you have one, and a couple of questions and answers. Publish. The town appears in the footer and on the county page automatically.

---

## How to write a blog post

Sidebar → **Blog posts** → **+**. Title, Generate the slug, set **Published** to today, write the summary and the text, add a featured photo. Optional: pick the **Related service** so the post links to that service page. Publish.

---

## Things that are good to know

- **Publishing takes a minute or two** to show on the live site. Refresh the page.
- **Red warnings** under a field mean something is missing or too long (for example a title over 60 characters). You can still publish, but it is better to fix them.
- **SEO tab:** every page has a Title tag and Meta description for Google. Leave them empty to use the page title and summary automatically.
- **Do not delete** the Site settings, Home page or About page documents. Edit them instead.
- **If something looks wrong**, use Restore in the **⋮** menu to go back to the last published version, then contact TTR Digital.
- **If the website ever cannot reach the editor**, it keeps showing the last published content, so visitors never see an empty page.
