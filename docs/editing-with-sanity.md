# Editing the Alpha Hardscapes website

Your website reads its text, photos and settings from a content editor called **Sanity Studio**. You edit in the Studio, press **Publish**, and the live site updates within a couple of minutes. No code, no developer needed.

**Where:** `https://alphahardscapes.com/studio` (sign in with the Google account or email TTR Digital invited).

Everything you change is saved as a draft until you press **Publish**, so you can take your time.

> **Before you change words on a main page, ask TTR first.** Each service, town and blog page ranks on Google for a search (for example "retaining walls" or "masonry avon"). That search has to stay in the page's title, main heading and first sentence. TTR checks new wording with you before it goes live.

## The sidebar

| Item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Site settings** | Phone number, email, town and ZIP, opening hours, areas served, social links, the main menu and footer menu, the forms (fields, thank-you page) and the blog author bio |
| **Pages** | Every page of the site, at the same address as before: home, services, towns and counties, gallery, about, reviews, FAQ, contact, thank-you pages and ad pages |
| **Blog posts** | The articles under /blog/ (and the older ones under /uncategorized/) |
| **Blog list pages** | The blog page, the category pages and the author pages. The list of posts fills in by itself. |

## How a page is built

A page has a **Main photo** at the top and **Page content** below it. Page content is a list of **sections** from top to bottom. Each section holds **blocks**:

| Block | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Heading** | A heading. Level 1 is the page's main heading (only one per page, it is in the first section). Level 2 starts a new topic, 3 and 4 are smaller. |
| **Text** | Paragraphs, bullet lists, bold, italic and links |
| **Button** | A button that links to a page or opens a quote form |
| **Feature box** | The small titled boxes shown in a grid ("Local Expertise", "Insured"...) |
| **Check list** | A list with green dots |
| **FAQ** | Questions and answers (they open when clicked, and Google can show them in results) |
| **Reviews** | Customer reviews, shown as text |
| **Photo / Photo gallery** | Your project photos |
| **Quote form** | One of the site's forms, shown on the page |
| **Map**, **Video**, **Embed (HTML)** | Maps, videos and the GoHighLevel forms on the ad pages |

The site chooses the layout by itself from the blocks in a section (for example a heading, text and one photo sit side by side; the last section with a button becomes the dark band at the bottom).

## How to change text

1. Open the page (sidebar → **Pages** → for example *Paver Patios & Walkways*).
2. Open the section, click the block and type.
3. Press **Publish** (bottom right). Done.

To undo, open the **⋮** menu next to Publish and choose **Discard changes** (before publishing) or **Restore** a previous version (after).

## How to add or change photos

Open a **Photo** or **Photo gallery** block and drag photos in (or click **Select**). Fill in **Alt text**: one sentence that says what the photo shows, for example *"Bluestone walkway with granite edging to a front porch"*.

Use only your own photos of real jobs (patios, walls, walkways, steps, pools). Please no stock photos, AI images or small odd jobs. Avoid house numbers and faces unless the customer agreed. Phone photos are fine; the site resizes them.

## How to change the phone number, hours or email

Sidebar → **Site settings** → **Business**.

- **Phone (as shown)** is what people see: `(475) 227-6896`. **Phone (for tap-to-call)** is what phones dial: `+14752276896`. Change both.
- **Town** and **ZIP** are what the site and Google show as your location. Never enter a street address.

Press **Publish**. The header, footer, mobile call bar and the business information Google reads all update.

## How to change a form

Sidebar → **Site settings** → **Forms**. Each form has its fields, its button text and its **Thank-you page**. Keep the field labels as they are unless TTR says otherwise: they are the names your CRM (GoHighLevel) receives.

## How to write a blog post

Sidebar → **Blog posts** → **+**. Fill in the **Title**, the **Address** (`/blog/your-post-name/`), **Published** date, author, a short **Summary** and a **Featured photo**. Write the post in **Page content** with one **Text** block (use Heading 2 and Heading 3 for sub-headings). If the post ends with questions, put them under a Heading 2 called "FAQ" with each question as a Heading 3 ending in "?" so Google can read them. Publish.

## The SEO tab

Every page has an **SEO** tab with the **Title tag** (what Google shows as the blue link) and **Meta description** (the grey text under it). These were copied from the old site. Please do not change them without asking TTR.

## Things that are good to know

- **Publishing takes a minute or two** to show on the live site. Refresh the page.
- **Do not change a page's Address.** Old links and Google rankings point to it.
- **Red warnings** under a field mean something is missing or too long. You can still publish, but it is better to fix them.
- **Do not delete** Site settings or a page. Edit it instead, or ask TTR.
- **If something looks wrong**, use Restore in the **⋮** menu to go back to the last published version, then contact TTR Digital.
- **If the website ever cannot reach the editor**, it keeps showing its built-in copy of the content, so visitors never see an empty page.
