Fonts/colors/platform come from the raw HTML (Google Fonts/Typekit links, CSS `font-family`, hex-color frequency, generator tags), so they are evidence-based but not a full visual audit. ## Patterns

### Recurring patterns across the set (and who does them best)

1. **Phone in the header plus a repeated "consultation/estimate" button.** 15 of 21 sites put the number in the header; those that hide it in the footer (Dex by Terra, Platinum, Absolute, Carter, Landscape Creations, Pristine Acres) all score lower on lead-gen regardless of design. Best: Burkholder (6 tel links), Sponzilli (floating Call Now + Get a Quote), NOVA (phone + "Request an Estimate" button side by side).
2. **A named, numbered process (3-8 steps).** Premier Pavers (3), Absolute (3), Dex by Terra (3), Monello (4), Groff (4 with 24-hour response), Burkholder (8). Process sections appear on every site scoring 7+ on lead-gen. Three steps with plain-English names ("Share Your Vision / We Handle the Details / Enjoy") read best.
3. **Specific commitments beat adjectives.** The highest-converting copy states numbers: Groff's "Timelines and Budgets Guaranteed", Burkholder's "same-day reply before 5 pm", Earth Turf & Wood's "Free 1-Hour Consultation", NOVA's "Class A license, ICPI, NCMA, fully insured", Premier's "1M+ sq ft of pavers installed".
4. **Town-level local SEO baked into the IA.** Burkholder (27-town dropdown), Earth Turf & Wood (8 city pages), Pearl (service-area footer links + 4 offices), Platinum and Sponzilli (county/town landing pages + geo blog), Groff (8 service areas in footer). Sites without this (Stephens, Carter, Landscape Creations) rely on referrals.
5. **Project cards with town captions and detail pages, not lightbox-only galleries.** Monello, Landshapes, Sponzilli, Cipriano, Willow Gates and Rhine show "[Title] – [Town, ST]"; Burkholder, Earth Turf & Wood and Platinum lose that value by opening raw images or omitting captions. Only Cipriano offers true filtering of case studies; Burkholder and Rhine filter photos by category.
6. **Before/after exists but is under-used.** Borst (narrative + Before/After groups), Pearl (category), Carter (dedicated gallery). Nobody in the set uses a slider widget; it is an open opportunity.
7. **Financing and warranty as nav-level items.** Rhine (both), NOVA (both), Monello (financing badges with rates), Absolute, Premier Pavers (financing). Groff pairs a 10-year hardscape warranty with 0% financing. Absent from the luxury-positioned sites.
8. **Manufacturer badges are rare.** Only Platinum Group (Unilock Authorized Contractor) and Gappsi (list of paver brands) show them; NOVA shows ICPI/NCMA. For Alpha, a Unilock/Techo-Bloc/Belgard authorized badge plus ICPI would stand out in CT, where buyers know the brands.
9. **Reviews are mostly 1-3 hand-picked quotes with town names.** Only Sponzilli embeds a live Google rating with a count (4.1 / 38); Absolute runs an Elfsight widget. A live aggregate rating is still a differentiator in this category.
10. **Audience segmentation on the homepage.** Dex by Terra (Res/Com toggle), Monello (segmented CTAs), Absolute (Homeowners/Commercial), Pristine Acres and Landscape Creations (Homeowners / Builders / Architects). Useful for Alpha if commercial or builder work matters; otherwise a distraction.
11. **Secondary conversions for not-ready visitors.** Groff's "Guide to Choosing a Design/Build Company", Cipriano's backyard guide, Burkholder's Pricing page, Gappsi's text-message link, newsletters (Carter, Groff, NOVA, Pearl). Only Groff's is current and well integrated.
12. **Agency fingerprints.** The industry-specialist agencies (Halstead: Premier Pavers, Platinum; Ramblin Jackson: Burkholder; Oostas: Earth Turf & Wood) produce the most consistent conversion architecture; the custom studio builds (Alchemy + Aim for Absolute, Webflow for Landscape Creations) produce the best design.

### What correlates with the highest scores

- **Highest combined scores (Premier Pavers 16, Groff 16, Absolute 15, Burkholder 15, Sponzilli 14, Landscape Creations 14, Pristine Acres 15 but lopsided):** all have a visible process, a stated guarantee or proof number, project pages (not only lightboxes), and a repeated single primary CTA with consistent wording. The two with both scores >= 8 (Premier Pavers, Groff-ish) use one sans family with a disciplined palette (Inter + charcoal; Josefin Sans + gold) and white space rather than sliders.
- **Design 8-9 without lead-gen (Landscape Creations, Stephens, Pristine Acres):** luxury positioning, no phone or form on the home page, soft CTAs. Great to borrow aesthetics and case-study storytelling from, but not the conversion model.
- **Lead-gen 8 without design (Rhine, NOVA, Sponzilli):** dense SEO copy, financing/warranty/reviews in nav, phone everywhere. Their trust stack is what Alpha should carry into a better-designed shell.

### Surprises

- Not one site uses a sticky mobile call/estimate bar in the strict sense; Sponzilli's floating buttons are the closest. That is a cheap win for Alpha.
- Before/after sliders, the most-cited "best practice" in design roundups, are absent from all 21 real sites; the few that do before/after use static side-by-side groups.
- Several otherwise strong firms have homepage problems that would never pass a content review: counters rendering "0" (Monello), a hiring banner as the first screen (Carter, Willow Gates), 2016-2022 copyright dates (Platinum, Dex, Landshapes), a 404 Portfolio link (Pristine Acres), mismatched phone numbers (Cipriano) and inconsistent years-in-business (Borst, Pearl, Candide). Simple hygiene is a competitive edge.
- Serif or script display type is common at the luxury end (Playfair at Monello, Libre Baskerville at Carter, Cinzel + Alex Brush at Earth Turf & Wood, mastro-display at Pristine Acres), while the two best all-round sites use a single geometric sans (Inter, Josefin Sans). Alpha can go either way; the discipline matters more than the category.
- Seasonal urgency ("Winter is design season, get on our schedule") appears on only one site (Burkholder) despite being an obvious fit for a Northeast build calendar.

### Shortlist for the Alpha Hardscapes redesign brief

- Structure and conversion: Groff (guarantees, lead magnet, 24-hr SLA, attribution field), Burkholder (town nav, pricing page, seasonal banner), NOVA (credential sentence, warranty + financing pages).
- Visual system: Premier Pavers (Inter, stats bar, airy grid), Absolute Landscapes (hero formula, 3-step process), Pearl (dark photo sections) and Landscape Creations/Pristine Acres (case-study narrative with real numbers).
- Portfolio model: Monello/Sponzilli card grid with "[Title] – [Town, CT]" captions and detail pages, plus Cipriano-style category filters and a Borst-style before/after section.
