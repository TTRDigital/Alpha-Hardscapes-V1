# Connecticut Competitor Website Audit — Hardscape / Masonry / Outdoor Living

Research date: 2026-11-23. Prepared for the alphahardscapes.com redesign (Cheshire, CT; New Haven / Hartford / Fairfield Counties).

Method: WebSearch for town + service queries (Cheshire, Southington, Wallingford, Hamden, Wolcott, Waterbury, Meriden, Farmington Valley, Glastonbury, West Hartford, shoreline, Fairfield County), filtered to real company sites (directories such as Angi, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, BBB, Thumbtack were used only to locate company URLs). Each site: homepage fetched plus one inner page (services, gallery, projects or a town page). Fetches return text only, so typography/colour notes are inferred from structure, copy and platform; visual polish scores lean on layout, hierarchy, imagery depth and copy quality. Three sites (Lions Creek, Excellence Masonry, Outdoor Design & Living) render client-side or returned 503s and are only partially assessed.

Note on Alpha's current site (alphahardscapes.com, WordPress + Elementor, built by TTR Digital Marketing): hero form (Name/Phone/Email/Project Address), 7-step "why us", 5-step process, 8-image uncaptioned project grid, Google review carousel with no aggregate, county-level service-area pages only (no town pages), "We're insured" but no HIC number, no financing, no manufacturer badges, no before/after. That baseline is referenced in the "Alpha should do better" lines below.

## Common local trust signals and legal notes observed

**Connecticut Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration**
- CT DCP requires home improvement contractors to register and to display the HIC registration number on all advertising, business cards, signage and contracts (seen cited on directory pages; also stated by the Thumbtack/Houzz listings that show "License type: Home Improvement Contractor in CT").
- Sites that display it (usually footer, sometimes header bar): Bahler Brothers (HIC-CT 00554297, HIC-MA 128099), Royal Oak (HIC# 0548653 plus DEEP Reg B-1080 for pesticide/irrigation work), J&H (CT-HIC#0649200), Northend (HIC 0702533), Patios by Anthony (HIC #0667349), Chaput ("CT License #583627" in header bar). Off-site only: Lions Creek (HIC.0650496 on Houzz), Ibarra (HIC.0702949), Clarke and PetrowGardens list a number on Houzz but not on their sites.
- Roughly 60% of competitor sites show no HIC number at all (AQ, Pete's, Torrison, Marini, Elite, Hoffman, Glengate, CT Stonescaping, D.W. Burr, HQ, TJB, Mimmo). Alpha currently shows none either — displaying "CT HIC #______" in header or footer, on the contact page and in the form confirmation is a cheap, compliant differentiator.
- Typical wording patterns: "Fully Insured, CT License #…", "CT Licensed & Fully Insured", "Family-owned, licensed and insured", "Fully insured for peace of mind". Note that CT uses "registration" for HIC; several sites loosely say "licensed". Safer copy: "Registered Connecticut Home Improvement Contractor HIC.#… · Fully insured (general liability and workers' compensation)".

**Insurance wording**
- Most say "fully insured" without specifics. None publish certificate-of-insurance availability. Offering "COI available on request" and naming GL + workers' comp is more credible than "fully insured" alone.

**Warranty wording**
- AQ: 3-year workmanship warranty + Techo-Pro manufacturer backing. ATS: 5-year patio warranty. TJB: 10-year installation warranty. Bahler: warranties on hardscape installs, lighting and materials, plus a Warranty & Service page. Unilock Authorized Contractor program carries a 2-year Unilock-backed guarantee (per Unilock) — a useful badge if Alpha qualifies.

**Manufacturer / industry badges seen**
- ICPI certified installer (Torrison, Chaput, AQ, Bahler per directories, Jerry's Pathways). Techo-Pro (AQ). Nicolock Hardscape Pro (TJB). Logos: Techo-Bloc, Unilock, Nicolock, Belgard, Cambridge, Versa-Lok, Rinox, Ideal, U-TEC. NDS certified (drainage, TJB). BBB A+ (Royal Oak, TJB, Hoffman).
